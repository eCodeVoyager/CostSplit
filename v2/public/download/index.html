CostSplit

Flatmates, bills, bazar. Sorted. Works offline.

Download for Android Open the web app instead

Android 7 or newer

On a computer? Scan this with your Android phone to download the app there.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open CostSplit, start your home and share the invite link with your flatmates.

Updating later: the app tells you when a new version is out. Install it over the old one; your data stays.

What it does

  • Monthly billsRent, electricity, gas, internet, maid. Set once, tap Pay each month.
  • Bazar and mealsSplit groceries equally or by meals eaten, with a live meal rate.
  • Works with no signalEverything saves on your phone first and syncs when you're online. Sign in with just your email.

iPhone? Open the web app in Safari, tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen”.
